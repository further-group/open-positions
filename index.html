Trial & Compensation Terms

Please review these terms carefully before deciding whether to participate.

5-Task Trial

The introductory trial consists of 5 promotional tasks. These may include liking selected content, saving posts, watching short videos and confirming completion.

After all 5 trial tasks are completed and approved under the stated verification criteria, the trial payment is £5.

Training

After the trial, participants who choose to continue may complete 20 guided training tasks. Completion of the trial or training does not by itself guarantee acceptance into the full programme or future task availability.

Full Programme Earnings

The stated estimated earning range for eligible participants is £100–£300 per day.

This range is an estimate and is not guaranteed income. Actual earnings depend on task availability, eligibility, accepted and completed work, and the applicable rate or commission structure. Full payment terms should be provided in writing before any paid work is accepted.

Referral Programme

Eligible participants may receive a referral link and may qualify for referral commission under the applicable programme terms. Referral income is not guaranteed.

Participation basis: The exact working arrangement, eligibility requirements, payment schedule and any applicable contractor or employment status should be confirmed in writing before participation begins.
